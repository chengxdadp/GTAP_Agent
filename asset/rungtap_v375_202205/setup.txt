This program installs RunGTAP to your hard disk.

It places shortcuts to the RunGTAP program on your desktop
and on the Start Menu.

When you first start RunGTAP, please spend a little time
looking through the on-line help. Also print out the
tutorial Ho2K.DOC which will be found in the RunGTAP
directory.

The RunGTAP installation may contain 3rd party components
or DLL libraries which may only be redistributed in
compliance with the conditions imposed by their creators.
Such 3rd party components must not be disassembled or
reverse engineered. By clicking on INSTALL you agree to
these terms.
